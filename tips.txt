Welcome to the tips window! 
Here you will find some tips to help you get started!

Looping:
-
You can loop a section of an audio file by right-clicking the waveform and dragging.
To remove the looped section, simply right-click anywhere on the waveform.

-

Exporting Stems feature and Hyper-loading:
-
Exporting stems requires you to click "EXPORT FOLDER" first and foremost and then confirming the desired location of where you want to save your stems.

After you have exported the stems to an audio file you can Hyper-load it.
To Hyper-load an audio file, click on "LOCATE STEMS", then proceed to load an audio file that you already have the stems saved to.
